### Gallo Glass

**Problem**: The warehouse receives clean tier sheets to separate glass bottles when palletizing. The often stick and aren't squared, in order for machines to operate properly tier sheets must be square to ensure packaging efficiency. Initial idea was core drill, install bollards and a back plate to allow forklifts to square up tier sheets. 

**Goal**: Design something structurally strong enough to resist forklift impact. Allow for easy removal for future automation project. Reduce price of the initial proposal.

**Outcome**: Reduce down time for install, no need for shop shutdown. Cut price by a third of original proposal.

### Senior Capstone

**Problem**: Your team will define an issue of your choice, identify what you are trying to solve, design and manufacture a solution to your defined issue. all must be feasible (design, build, test, cost) in a year of your undergraduate degree.

**Goal**: My team and I decided to tackle an issue relevant to the medical, agricultural and food service field. Latex gloves ripping during donning process. We worked on the consistent application of a glove without room for human error. We designed and 3D printed parts and mounts for all electrical/mechanical components. Tested, iterated and verified each part and hand calculation.

My primary role in the project was assist with the design of the parts (GD&T) as well as some of the manufacturing. Test electrical components, and assemble the machine for further testing. I used my skills in 3D design, material selection, and hands on technical knowledge to push this project forward.

**Outcome**: Finalized a prototype to showcase toward the end of our semester. We gained experience, testing, reiterating, understanding engineering fundamentals/principles.
